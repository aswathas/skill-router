## 2026-11-24 — the semantic layer

| File | What changed | Why | How tested |
|---|---|---|---|
| `skills/aswath-capability/scripts/embed.py` | New: the embedding client and index. Local OpenAI-compatible provider (LM Studio, `text-embedding-nomic-embed-text-v1.5`), base64 float32 packing, mtime-memoised cache, and a `build` that embeds every skill plus a centroid per capability | 42 of 45 routing failures were requests that never named the capability, so the router needed a way to infer meaning without a shared word | `embed.py build` -> 3,015 skills and 79 centroids in 12.6 MB, 15 s; `embed.py status` loads in 0.96 s cold, 0.000 s warm |
| `skills/aswath-capability/scripts/semantic.py` | New: the layer itself. `rescue` (one capability voted by the nearest skill, only when the keywords said nothing) and `tie` (nearest skill wins among the carriers of the winning capability). Two profiles, conservative by default, and a keyword fallback when the provider is down | The failures are inference misses and sibling ties; both need the semantic signal, and both were measured before they were kept | `evals/run_semantic_eval.py`: conservative keeps capability top-1 at 0.805, lifts strict top-1 0.206 -> 0.222 and cuts mean cost 2435 -> 2096; aggressive lifts strict to 0.254 for 3 capability cases lost |
| `evals/run_semantic_eval.py` | New: measures the layer against the shipped router on the same 298 cases, with McNemar on the strict subset, writing to `evals/local/` | The paper's `evals/results.json` is evidence and must not be overwritten by a local run | Prints both rows and the discordant pairs; writes `evals/local/semantic.json`; `evals/results.json` untouched |
| `evals/exp_semantic.py` | New: the experiment that found the shape of the answer. | `tests/test_semantic.py` | New: seven tests over vector packing, cosine, the shipped profile, the off switch, the prefilter and the dead-provider fallback | The layer has branches that fail silently | `pytest tests` -> 54 passed |
